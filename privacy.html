Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Summary

The Extension has no server of its own. It does not collect, transmit or sell your data to the author or to any third party other than TikTok, and it contains no analytics or advertising.

Data stored on your device

  • TikTok app settings you enter (client key, client secret, optional token endpoint, chunk size).
  • TikTok OAuth tokens (access token, refresh token, open ID) issued after you approve access.
  • A history of your uploads: file name, size, SHA-256 checksum, TikTok publish ID and status.

This data is kept in your browser profile (chrome.storage) and never leaves your device except as described below. It is not encrypted by the Extension.

Data sent to TikTok

To perform the upload the Extension sends requests to TikTok's servers (tiktokapis.com): your access token for authorisation, and the video file you chose, in unmodified form. TikTok's handling of that data is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy.

Video files

Video files are read locally in your browser to analyse them and to compute checksums. They are sent only to TikTok as described above and are never copied elsewhere.

Your controls

  • "Disconnect" in the Extension revokes and deletes the stored tokens.
  • Removing the Extension deletes all data it stored.
  • You can also revoke the app's access in your TikTok account settings.

Children

The Extension is not directed at children under 13.
